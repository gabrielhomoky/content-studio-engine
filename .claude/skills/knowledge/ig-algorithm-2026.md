# Instagram distribution notes (guidance, not verified statistics)

Instagram changes ranking often and publishes only partial explanations. Treat these as
working principles that shape scripts; never quote them as hard numbers to the client, and
let OBSERVED data from the research run win when it disagrees.

## Signals that tend to matter

- **Sends / shares (especially via DM)**: widely described by Instagram as a key signal for
  reaching non-followers. Design posts people forward to a partner or a friend who is
  renovating ("send this to whoever is choosing the paint").
- **Saves**: strong for useful content (lists, codes, sizes, guides).
- **Watch time and retention**: the first ~3 seconds decide whether a reel is shown further.
  Completion and rewatches (loops) help; short, dense reels loop better.
- **Likes per reach** and **comments** matter, mostly within the existing audience.
- **Originality**: original content is favoured over reposts/aggregated clips; avoid
  watermarks from other apps.

## Practical implications

- Hook + first frame get most of the effort. Test 2-3 hook variants on the same footage.
- **Trial reels** (shown first to non-followers) are a low-risk way to test hooks or new
  formats without affecting followers' feeds.
- **Carousels**: adding music can let them surface in more places; multiple slides give
  multiple chances (a second slide may be re-shown).
- **Captions and on-screen text are searchable**: use natural keywords, location terms and
  the words clients actually type.
- **Consistency beats bursts**: a steady cadence (for example 3 reels + 2 carousels a week)
  with daily stories keeps distribution warm; long gaps cool the account.
- Stories do not bring new people but convert existing followers (polls, question boxes,
  behind the scenes, "we have one slot in {month}").
- Reply to comments in the first hour; comment threads extend distribution.
- Pin 3 posts that do the work for profile visitors: best before/after, the process/offer
  post, the strongest story/credibility post.
- Profile: clear bio (who, where, what outcome), one link, highlights that answer
  "portfolio · process · reviews · how to work with me".
