# Interior design niche playbook

For interior designers and studios whose Instagram should bring (a) reach and (b) premium
clients. Everything here is guidance distilled from what tends to work in the niche; always
prefer the OBSERVED patterns of the current research run when they disagree.

## What premium clients respond to

- **Taste**: a recognisable point of view (colour, materials, humour, history) shown
  consistently. Clients hire a sensibility, not a service list.
- **Trust**: finished real projects, real clients, real constraints, awards and press shown
  calmly (as proof inside a story, not as bragging).
- **Process**: how decisions are made, how problems on site are solved, how the designer
  protects the client from expensive mistakes.
- **Results**: before/after, lived-in rooms, what the family now does in the space.
- **Calm confidence**: no begging for work, no discounts, no "DM me for prices". The tone of
  someone who is booked.

Reach content and lead content should not live in separate worlds: reach posts end with a
light bridge to the offer (profile, portfolio, "we take a few projects in {city} each year").

## Formats that work for designers

| Format | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Before/after reveal (reel) | reach | one clean transition; before must look genuinely worse |
| Designer rules / "N things I would never…" (reel or carousel) | reach + saves | show each rule on a real room |
| Mistake callout (reel) | shares | contrarian first line; offer the fix |
| Colour story with exact paint codes (carousel or reel) | saves | codes, brand, room, light direction, pairings |
| Walkthrough / home tour with voiceover (reel) | trust | calm pace, one decision per room |
| Process / behind the scenes on site (reel, stories) | trust | problems solved, self-irony, real people |
| Client story "they wanted X, we did Y" (reel or carousel) | trust + leads | anonymise unless allowed |
| "What €X buys" / budget vs luxury (reel) | reach | only real prices; stays premium by explaining value, not cheapness |
| POV reels (designer/homeowner moments) | reach | relatable, short, humour |
| ASMR details (reel) | reach, loops | materials, doors, textiles, stone, light |
| "Save this" guide: sizes, heights, distances (carousel) | saves | real numbers |
| Award/press moment told as a story (carousel) | trust | the journey, not the trophy |
| Travel & inspiration → design idea (carousel) | brand | tie every trip to a design lesson or a project |

## Topic lanes to rotate

colour (her signature if colour-forward) · small spaces · kitchens & bathrooms (high search) ·
hallways/entrances (underrated, great before/after) · lighting · art & objects · materials ·
budget allocation · renovation mistakes · working with a designer (what it costs, how it
works, remote projects) · location lanes (e.g. Mediterranean homes, city apartments) ·
the designer's life (site days, sourcing trips, studio).

## Shooting on site with a phone (shot-list vocabulary)

Use these exact terms in `shot_list` so the client can film in one visit:
- **Reveal pan**: slow horizontal pan from a wall/detail to the full room.
- **Slow push-in**: walk or glide slowly towards the focal point (sofa, art, window).
- **Doorway reveal**: start behind a door frame, step through.
- **Detail macro**: 2-3 s close-ups of texture (fabric, stone, handle, paint edge).
- **Hand-in-frame**: a hand touching a material, opening a drawer, placing an object.
- **Transition cut**: same framing before/after, cut on a hand swipe or door close.
- **Top-down**: flat lay of samples/palette on a table.
- **Talking head**: phone at eye level, window light on the face, 1-2 lines max per clip.
- **B-roll loop**: 3-5 s of a calm, static, beautiful angle for voiceover.
Practical: vertical 9:16, 4K 30fps if possible, lock exposure, clean the lens, shoot each
move twice, morning or late-afternoon light, lights on in the room, no people unless agreed.

## Language and markets

When the client serves several markets (e.g. a home market audience in one language and an
international premium market in English), keep one voice and choose the language per post:
home-market stories in the home language; international portfolio, colour codes and
before/afters in English or bilingual (English on-screen text + home-language caption, or
the reverse). Let `context.prefs.default_script_lang` and the idea's `lang_hint` decide.

## Guardrails

- Never invent projects, clients, awards, press or prices. Placeholders in `[brackets]` when a
  fact is needed but unknown.
- Respect client privacy: no addresses, no faces/family details without consent.
- Premium brand: no cheap hacks, coupons, "dupes", or bargain energy.
