# Pattern mining and content gaps

Goal: compress many analysed breakouts into a few rules the client can act on, then find the
empty space.

## How to mine

1. **Tabulate** every breakdown: format, hook type, hook mechanism, topic, structure, CTA,
   breakout.
2. **Count what recurs.** A pattern shows up in roughly 30%+ of breakouts, or in the top few
   outliers specifically. One-offs are not patterns.
3. **Separate common from winning.** A format everybody uses is not automatically the one that
   breaks out. Weight by breakout.
4. **Write each pattern as a rule:** "Before/after reveal + spoken contrarian hook + save CTA
   over-indexes in this set." Put the supporting post ids in `examples` and the share of
   breakouts that show it in `share_pct` (0-100, computed from the tabulation).

Extract: hook patterns (3-5 mechanisms), format patterns (2-3), topic patterns, structural
patterns (pacing, reveal order, retention loops, CTA style).

## Content gaps (usually the best opportunities)

A gap = something the audience clearly wants that the competitor set serves badly.
- **Topic gap**: questions in comments nobody answers with a dedicated post.
- **Format gap**: a winning topic only done as a carousel, nobody made the reel (or vice versa).
- **Quality gap**: everybody covers it shallowly; depth or real codes/sizes would stand out.
- **Audience gap**: content made for designers, none for homeowners who will hire one (or the
  reverse); nothing for her specific markets and languages.
- **Angle gap**: everybody is polished and aspirational; an honest, self-ironic or
  contrarian take is open.

## The formula

End the patterns report with `formula`: 2-4 sentences telling the client exactly what her
next posts should combine (hook mechanism + format + topic lane + CTA), grounded in the rules
above and in what already works on her own account.

## Repurpose-into-10 (one proven idea → a sprint)

Vary one axis each time: hook mechanism · format (talking head → walkthrough → carousel) ·
narrower sub-topic · opposite stance · beginner vs advanced · story/case version · mistakes
version · tools/resources version (paint codes, suppliers) · reaction to a take in the niche ·
results version (real numbers only).

## Transform, never copy

Every borrowed idea changes at least one axis (audience, angle, format, medium, stance,
market, language) and says which in `remake_axis`. Never propose re-posting, translating
or re-shooting a competitor's video 1:1, and never lift their captions or their projects.
