# Carousel frameworks

Carousels win on **saves and dwell time**. They are the natural home for exhaustive lists,
codes, plans and before/after sequences. Reach-first topics should usually be tested as a
reel first; the carousel then becomes the "save this" companion.

## PIE (Problem · Insight · Execution): the default

Slide 1 carries the whole promise in three short lines:
```
PROBLEM    "Your living room looks unfinished."        (the surface pain, the mirror)
INSIGHT    "It is not the furniture. It is the light."  (the counter-intuitive real cause)
EXECUTION  "5 fixes that cost less than a sofa."        (the concrete promise, a reason to swipe)
```
Slides 2-N: one technique / rule / item per slide. Last slide: recap + one CTA.

## Other proven carousel shapes for interiors

- **Before → after sequence**: slide 1 = the after (best photo) with a hook line, slides 2-3 =
  before, then the key decisions one per slide, last slide = full after + CTA.
- **Colour story / palette card**: one room, the palette with exact paint names and codes,
  where each colour sits, what not to pair it with. Highly saveable.
- **"Save this" guide**: sizes, distances, heights (sofa to coffee table, pendant over table,
  rug size by room). Real numbers only.
- **Project story**: the brief, the constraint, the decision, the result. Diary tone works
  for a personal-brand designer: honest, specific, a little self-ironic.
- **Mistakes list**: "5 things I would never do in a small kitchen", each slide with the fix.
- **Room anatomy**: one photo repeated with a callout per slide (lamp, rug, art, textile).

## Slide rules

- 5-10 slides (up to 20 are allowed; do not pad).
- Slide 1 is 80% of the result: a strong image + max ~10-12 words of large text.
- One idea per slide, readable in 2 seconds on a phone.
- Consistent typography and margins; text never over busy parts of the photo.
- Add music to photo carousels (it can make them eligible for more reel-like distribution;
  treat as guidance, not guarantee).
- Last slide: CTA (save / send to a friend who is renovating / comment WORD / enquire).

## Output per slide

`{n, headline, body, visual}`: `visual` says exactly which photo/shot and how to frame it
("wide shot of the green hallway from the front door, straight on, morning light").
